Scenes from a storyboard

llmctl storyboard plan.json makes a scene from a photo: keyframes with Qwen-Image Edit, four LTX-2.5 clips through them, checks before and after rendering, and a final video with one soundtrack. The beach scene below is examples/storyboard.json; one --render makes it (~2 h).

The finished scene

A woman walks to a shell lying in the sand, picks it up, holds it to her ear and listens; the close-up rises out of the wide shot in a 0.75 s dissolve, and she listens on, opens her eyes and smiles. 20 s, rendered from the plan alone: the chain, then clip 4 with a guide picture in its middle. The sound is one track of surf and wind from Stable Audio, not LTX's per clip.

The same scene to a song

"soundtrack": "song.flac" lays a song under the storyboard instead of the ambience: one continuous scene to music. The song is a 30 s instrumental from Stable Audio 3 (guitar, piano, strings).

Keyframes

Every keyframe is edited from the photo (or from the keyframe before, where the person must stay in place) with Qwen-Image Edit Turbo. The shell is pasted in at one fixed place until it is picked up; a vision model and SAM 3 check positions and sizes before an hour of rendering.

Seven rounds, and what each taught

Each version fixed what the one before showed:

  1. v1 — a giant shell, picked up, put down again, picked up: the keyframe after an action was a moment inside it.
  2. v2 — resting-state keyframes, a small shell; at pull 0.5 a ghost at every clip's end.
  3. v3 — the shell pasted in where it lies, pull 0.7: every clip reaches its keyframe; a stall before 10 s.
  4. v4 — clip lengths of their own; clip 3 had her walk back where she did not face, and LTX dissolved her there.
  5. v5 — "brushes the sand off" became a sand fountain with a small figure floating out of it.
  6. v6 — clean of ghosts and jumps; the sea did not move.
  7. v7 — other waves in every keyframe: the sea moves.

How hard to pull a clip to its keyframe

Clip 1 three times, the walk to the shell, with Stärke Zielbild 0.5, 0.7 and 1.0. At 0.5 she rocks in place and jumps to the end picture in its last frame; at 0.7 she walks there and stands still for 1.2 s; at 1.0 the shell flies in through the air. The shell moved at all because Qwen had drawn it elsewhere in each keyframe — hence pasting it in.
